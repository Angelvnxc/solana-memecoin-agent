# Stage Requirements

## Purpose

This document defines the requirements the agent must satisfy before
advancing through its learning and trading lifecycle.

The agent must demonstrate understanding through practical evaluation,
not simply through stored information.

The agent must never skip a stage.

---

# Stage 0 — Birth

## Capabilities

The agent may:

- read
- research
- learn
- store information
- analyze educational examples
- explain concepts

The agent may not:

- buy tokens
- sell tokens
- sign trading transactions
- move funds
- manage real positions

## Advancement Requirements

The agent must demonstrate that it understands:

- what Solana is
- what a wallet is
- what a token is
- what a transaction is
- what a DEX is
- what liquidity means
- what market capitalization means
- what trading volume means
- what holders are
- what slippage means
- what a memecoin is

The agent must also understand that learning comes before trading.

---

# Stage 1 — Solana & Memecoin Fundamentals

## Capabilities

The agent can study real Solana market information.

It cannot trade.

## Required Knowledge

The agent must understand:

- Solana architecture at a practical level
- wallets and key ownership
- token accounts
- transactions
- fees
- DEXs
- liquidity
- trading pairs
- market capitalization
- token supply
- circulating supply
- volume
- slippage
- price impact
- holders
- liquidity providers
- token launches
- memecoin mechanics
- narratives
- basic market structure

## Evaluation

The agent must correctly explain these concepts and identify them in
real examples.

It must also demonstrate that two tokens with similar market
capitalization can have completely different risk profiles.

---

# Stage 2 — Market Observation

## Capabilities

The agent may observe the live market.

It may identify potential opportunities.

It may not trade.

## Required Abilities

The agent must be able to:

- observe price behavior
- interpret volume in context
- evaluate liquidity
- observe holder changes
- identify unusual wallet activity
- identify emerging narratives
- recognize strong and weak market conditions
- distinguish observation from action

## Evaluation

The agent receives unseen market situations.

For each situation it must determine:

- interesting
- uncertain
- weak
- dangerous
- irrelevant

The agent must explain why.

No trading is permitted during this stage.

---

# Stage 3 — Deep Research

## Capabilities

The agent may investigate individual tokens in depth.

It still cannot trade.

## Required Abilities

The agent must be able to investigate:

- token history
- price structure
- liquidity
- volume
- holder distribution
- wallet behavior
- developer activity
- smart money activity
- on-chain activity
- social activity
- narratives
- broader market conditions

The agent must combine information from multiple sources.

## Evaluation

The agent receives several candidate tokens.

It must:

1. Research each candidate.
2. Identify the strongest evidence.
3. Identify contradictory evidence.
4. Identify unknown information.
5. Rank research priority.
6. Reject candidates that do not justify further investigation.

The agent must not treat every candidate equally.

---

# Stage 4 — Thesis Formation

## Capabilities

The agent can construct complete trading theses.

It still cannot trade.

## Required Thesis Components

Every thesis must contain:

- opportunity description
- reasoning
- supporting evidence
- contradicting evidence
- uncertainty
- bull scenario
- base scenario
- bear scenario
- risks
- invalidation conditions
- expected trade logic

## Evaluation

The agent receives unseen market situations.

It must construct a coherent thesis and identify conditions that would
invalidate it.

The agent must demonstrate that it can reject an opportunity when the
evidence is insufficient.

---

# Stage 5 — Paper Trading

## Capabilities

The agent may simulate trading with virtual capital.

No real funds may be used.

## Required Abilities

The agent must demonstrate:

- entry selection
- position sizing
- thesis tracking
- position monitoring
- profit taking
- loss management
- thesis invalidation
- exit reasoning
- trade journaling

## Evaluation

Paper trades must be evaluated across multiple market conditions.

The agent should demonstrate that it can:

- avoid chasing extreme moves
- avoid entering without a thesis
- exit when the thesis is invalidated
- take profits when appropriate
- remain inactive when no opportunity exists

Performance must not be judged only by profit.

Decision quality is also evaluated.

---

# Stage 6 — Evaluation

## Purpose

The agent is tested using situations it has not previously encountered.

The objective is to determine whether the agent can generalize its
reasoning rather than memorize patterns.

## Evaluation Areas

The agent must demonstrate:

- coherent research
- evidence evaluation
- uncertainty recognition
- thesis construction
- risk recognition
- invalidation detection
- disciplined execution logic
- ability to avoid overconfidence
- ability to reject weak opportunities
- ability to do nothing

## Failure Conditions

The agent must not advance if it repeatedly:

- trades without sufficient evidence
- ignores contradictory evidence
- chases pumps
- relies on one metric
- confuses attention with opportunity
- treats low market capitalization as automatic value
- treats high volume as automatic buying pressure
- ignores invalidation
- overreacts to individual outcomes
- trades simply to remain active

---

# Stage 7 — Real Trading

## Entry Requirements

Real trading may only be enabled after all previous stages have been
completed and approved.

The agent must have demonstrated:

- research ability
- thesis formation
- risk assessment
- paper trading experience
- evaluation performance
- correct use of invalidation
- disciplined decision making
- ability to remain inactive

## Restrictions

The agent may trade only:

- Solana assets
- memecoins
- spot positions

The agent may have only one open position.

The agent cannot:

- withdraw funds
- transfer funds to another wallet
- modify wallet restrictions
- bypass system safety controls

---

# Continuous Learning

Passing Stage 7 does not end the learning process.

The agent continues to learn from:

- completed trades
- failed theses
- successful theses
- research mistakes
- market changes
- new narratives
- new attack patterns
- new wallet behaviors
- new market structures

Learning must continue throughout the agent's lifetime.

---

# Advancement Principle

Advancement should depend on demonstrated capability.

The agent should not advance simply because:

- enough time has passed
- enough tokens have been analyzed
- enough trades have been simulated
- a predetermined number of tasks were completed

Quality of reasoning matters more than quantity.

---

# Final Principle

The agent must earn the ability to trade.

It must first learn.

Then observe.

Then research.

Then reason.

Then simulate.

Then prove that it can make decisions under uncertainty.

Only then may it trade real capital.
